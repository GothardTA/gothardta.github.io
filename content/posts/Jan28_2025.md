# A new design
I have started to rework some of the designs for my connect four robot. There were problems with the old design that I felt like fixing. I looked at a design that someone made at MIT and took inspiration. I noticed they spin a part to grab a piece from the stack and move it to the dropper hole. I realized that my design should probably use that, my now old design used a servo and a stiff wire which caused it to not work a lot. For now I have made a new mechanism to grab a piece from the stack and move it into the dropper. Eventually I will rework how the board detection works. Instead of a camera, I want sensors to detect when I piece drops into a column. This will remove the need for a button to tell the computer to take a picture since the sensor will detect when the player plays a piece. For now this is what I have!
